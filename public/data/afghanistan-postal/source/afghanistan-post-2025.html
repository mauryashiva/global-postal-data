Afghanistan Coding Method

In 2024, Afghanistan introduced a new postcode system and domestic addressing standard. Under the new system, postcodes consist of six digits: the first two digits (ranging from 10–43) correspond to the province, the third and fourth digits correspond to the district or city, and the last two digits correspond to postal delivery zone. Afghanistan’s new postcode system is defined at the province level, but has been designed to easily incorporate future changes.

Please note that Afghanistan has recently implemented a new postal code system, and there may be overlaps between the old and new systems during this transition period. When filling in postal codes, it is advisable to confirm with the postal staff before proceeding.

Afghanistan Address Format

According to the new addressing standard, postcodes should be placed on a separate line after the province name and before the country name as follows: 

Jawad Ahmad Niazi 
16, Ziarat-e-Abolfazl St 
Murad Khani 
100208 KABUL
